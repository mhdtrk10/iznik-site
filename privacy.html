Privacy Policy

Iznik: Tile Bloom Puzzle · Effective date: October 30, 2026 · Türkçe

This policy explains what information the Iznik app ("the app") handles. In short: we don't ask you for anything, and we don't collect anything ourselves. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some data as described below.

No account, no personal information

The app has no account or sign-in and never asks for your name, email address or any other personal information. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt;
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address;
  • Ad interactions (for example, which ads were shown or tapped);
  • Diagnostic data (for example, crash and performance information of the ads SDK).

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you decline tracking, you will still see ads, but they won't be personalized using your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Consent in the EU, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form provided by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before ads are personalized. You can review or change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy Settings.

In-app purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. Your payment information is handled by Apple and is never shared with the developer.

Children

The app is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
