Iznik

A calm, colorful tile puzzle inspired by the hand-painted tiles of Iznik.

Drag tiles onto the board. Every tile has a quarter of a flower on each corner. When four tiles meet with the same color, the flower blooms and the tiles clear away. No timer, no rush — just one more move.
